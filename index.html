Design 01 of 05

Centred Column

Accordion
Category pills sit centred in a row above a single column of questions. Each question is an outlined card; one opens at a time, the open card fills mint and its plus sign turns into a green minus.
Enquire Now
Inside the answers. On MICE and Weddings, every opened answer ends with an Enquire Now button, with the phone number beside it.

Design 02 of 05

Stacked Topics

Accordion
Two levels, no tabs. Each category is a bordered row with an icon and question count; opening it reveals its questions underneath. One category and one question open at a time, with thin lines between questions.
Enquire Now
On the category row. MICE and Weddings show Enquire Now on the right of their row, so it is visible even before the category is opened. On phones it drops below the category name.

Design 03 of 05

Mint Panel

Accordion
Categories are listed in a sticky mint panel on the left; choosing one shows its questions on the right. Questions are numbered 01, 02…, one opens at a time and the open question gets a mint background.
Enquire Now
In the green card under the category list. When MICE or Weddings is selected, the card changes to an enquiry message with Enquire Now; for the other categories it shows Contact Us.

Design 04 of 05

Side Tabs

Accordion
Sticky side tabs show each category’s number and question count; choosing one shows its questions on the right. Questions are numbered white cards with a thin outline; one opens at a time and the open card gets a mint outline and soft shadow.
Enquire Now
Beside the category title. On MICE and Weddings, Enquire Now sits to the right of the category title, above the questions.

Design 05 of 05

Contact Card

Accordion
The same two-level accordion as Design 02: each category row opens to show its questions, one at a time. A sticky contact card on the right shows phone, email and location; on phones it moves below the questions.
Enquire Now
In two places. On the MICE and Weddings category rows (as in Design 02), and in the contact card, which switches to an enquiry message with Enquire Now while MICE or Weddings is open.
